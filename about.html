About Us

Festive Initiative

Our festive initiative aims to provide a magical Christmas experience by tracing Santa Claus’s journey through Coral Gables. We want both residents and visitors to feel the holiday spirit and excitement as they discover the various spots in the city that Santa visits during his journey.

Community Spirit

Our project seeks to foster joy, unity, and a festive atmosphere within the community. We believe that these celebrations are an excellent opportunity for community members to come together, share special moments, and create unforgettable memories. Through community activities and events, we hope to strengthen bonds and a sense of belonging.

Interactive Experience

We offer an interactive experience suitable for all ages, featuring scenes of Santa Claus that capture the imagination of both children and adults. These scenes include fun and educational activities that allow participants to engage actively and enjoy an immersive and enchanting experience.

Multiple Routes

Our program includes different routes, such as Truck 1, Truck 2, and Truck 3, each offering varied experiences. Each route is designed to showcase different areas of Coral Gables, allowing participants to explore and enjoy diverse landscapes and holiday decorations. This ensures there is something new and exciting for everyone, no matter how many times they participate.
