Santa Claus Route for Christmas

The "Santa Claus Route for Christmas" project aims to provide residents with a magical and festive experience by representing the journey of Santa Claus and his reindeer through the city. This initiative seeks to foster the Christmas spirit, create an atmosphere of joy and unity within the community of Coral Gables, and offer an interactive and visually appealing activity for all ages.
